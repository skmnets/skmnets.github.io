### University of Central Florida

#### Graduate Teaching Assistant

- **CAP 6318: Computational Analysis of Social Complexity**  
  *Fall 2025, Fall 2026*  
  **Theoretical concepts:**
  - *Graphs:* Algorithmic graph analysis and network representations
  - *Game Theory:* Strategic interaction and competitive dynamics
  - *Agent-Based Models (ABM):* Simulation of complex rule-based systems
  - *Auction Theory:* Game theory at scale in bidding markets
  - *Blockchain:* Cryptographically secure, public, distributed ledgers
  - *Networks:* Social, information, and financial network structures  
  **Applications:** Twitter / social media dynamics, economic markets and trade networks, and computational social science.

- **CAP 6640: Computer Understanding of Natural Language** (*Master of Science in Data Analytics - MSDA Program*)  
  *Spring 2026*  
  **Core concepts:**
  - *Text processing and representation:* From raw text to meaningful features
  - *Classical NLP and machine learning techniques:* Foundations for language tasks
  - *Deep learning evolution:* Progression from RNNs to transformers to modern foundation models
  - *Application engineering:* Building production applications with modern LLM APIs and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) patterns
  - *Agentic AI systems:* Orchestrating LLMs with external tools, functions, and autonomous multi-step workflows  
  **Applications & Domains:** Machine translation and multilingual systems, chatbots and conversational AI, search and information retrieval, sentiment analysis and text classification, summarization, and question answering across tech, healthcare, finance, and legal domains.

- **STA 6704: Statistical Data Mining Methodology II** (*MSDA Program*)  
  *Summer 2025, Summer 2026*  
  Graduate teaching assistant and grader for advanced statistical data mining and predictive modeling.

- **STA 5206: Statistical Analysis**  
  *Fall 2025, Summer 2026*  
  Graduate teaching assistant for foundational graduate-level statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and regression analysis.

- **MSDA Bridge Program** (*Statistics and Computer Science*)  
  *Summer 2025*  
  Supported incoming graduate cohort bridging mathematical statistics and computing fundamentals.

- **Mathematics Success Center (MSC)**  
  *Fall 2024, Spring 2025*

- **Mathematics Assistance and Learning Lab (MALL)**  
  *Fall 2022, Spring 2023*
