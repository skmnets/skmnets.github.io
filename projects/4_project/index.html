Overview

Generating realistic molecular graphs via diffusion requires balancing structural chemical validity with generative novelty and sample diversity. Unconstrained edge diffusion frequently produces chemically invalid intermediates and slow convergence.

Key Contributions

  • Motif-Constrained Diffusion: Formulated discrete diffusion mechanisms constrained to preserve known biochemical substructures and motifs throughout the reverse process.
  • Biochemical Representations: Developed motif-aware molecular graph representations ensuring high validity, novelty, and chemical property targeting.
  • Computational Efficiency: Drastically reduced search spaces and diffusion steps compared to unconstrained atom-by-atom generative baselines.

Published at the ICML 2026 Workshop on Generative and Agentic AI for Biology.
