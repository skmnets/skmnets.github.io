### Overview

Discrete graph diffusion processes often obscure structural information at rates that depend intimately on network size and community geometry. This work provides an analytical bridge between discrete graph diffusion and continuous graphon dynamics.

### Key Contributions
- **Exact Graphon Trajectories**: Connected discrete edge corruption to continuous trajectories in $W$-graphon space, characterizing contraction rates of community and spectral modes.
- **Finite-Size SNR Coordinates**: Derived finite-size structural signal-to-noise coordinates linking graph size $n$, corruption level $t$, and reconstruction fidelity, establishing sharp, size-dependent information horizons for denoising.
- **Cross-Size Generalization**: Demonstrated zero-shot transfer of diffusion coordinates across different network scales, validated by held-out spectral audits and ablation experiments.
