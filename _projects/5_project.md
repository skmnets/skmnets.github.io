### Overview

Traditional methods for inferring gene regulatory networks (GRNs) focus on pairwise edge prediction, which struggles to capture multi-factor cooperative binding and combinatorial repression/activation complexes.

### Key Contributions
- **Regulator-Set Discovery**: Replaced pairwise classification with a two-stage residual set modeling framework designed to identify synergistic regulator teams directly.
- **Sparse Single-Cell Application**: Robust to high dropout and sparsity inherent in single-cell RNA-seq datasets, recovering mechanistically verified regulatory complexes.
- **ICLR 2026 Presentations**: Accepted and presented across three distinct workshop venues: **Gen²**, **MLGenX**, and **LMRL**.
