CV

PhD student in Industrial Engineering and Management Systems at the University of Central Florida and Graduate Research Assistant in the Complex Adaptive Systems Laboratory. Research interests include graph machine learning, graph limits, network science, sparse neural networks, and graph diffusion, with an emphasis on mathematical descriptions of information flow in learning systems.
